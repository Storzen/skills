# Demo — fixture family

A fixture, not a principle. It exists so the checker is proven to pass on a
correct v2 entry, not only to fail on an incorrect one.

- `UX-P90` — Fixture principle
- `UX-P91` — Fixture principle with a figure
- `UX-P92` — Fixture principle, retired — DEPRECATED

---

## UX-P90 — Fixture principle

**Cue.** a fixture screen needs one entry that satisfies every rule

**Principle.** One sentence: the actionable rule, not the theory.

**Mechanism.** Why the mind reacts this way, with no author and no year.

**Applies / doesn't.**
- ✅ Where the lever genuinely serves the user.
- ❌ Where it tips into a dark pattern.

**Ethical guard.** No meaningful abuse vector — a fixture changes no screen.

**Detection.** Absent: the fixture is missing. Weakened: the fixture is partial.

**Example.** A generic screen with no product-specific detail.

**Provenance.**
`standing:` replicated
`guard-basis:` independent of source
`origin:` Fixture 1900 — On Fixtures — book (academic) — https://example.org/fixture
`warrant:` Fixture 1950 — Fixtures Replicated — peer-reviewed — https://doi.org/10.1000/fixture

## UX-P91 — Fixture principle with a figure

**Cue.** a fixture screen states a quantity and must cite it

**Principle.** A response slower than 400 ms stops feeling instantaneous.

**Mechanism.** Why the mind reacts this way, with no author and no year.

**Applies / doesn't.**
- ✅ Where the lever genuinely serves the user.
- ❌ Where a fake "only 2 left" counter would be quoted screen copy, not a claim.

**Ethical guard.** Never manufacture the delay the lever is meant to remove.

**Detection.** Absent: no feedback. Weakened: feedback arrives late.

**Collides with.** `UX-P90` — the evidence favours this one for timing work.

**Example.** A save button that confirms before the user looks away.

**Provenance.**
`standing:` qualified (modern hardware)
`guard-basis:` stricter than source
`origin:` Doherty & Kelisky 1979 — Managing VM/CMS Systems — corporate technical report — https://example.org/doherty
`warrant:` Fixture 1950 — Fixtures Replicated — peer-reviewed — https://doi.org/10.1000/fixture
`figure:` "400 ms" — Doherty & Thadhani 1982
`current:` Fixture 2020 — What To Read Today — peer-reviewed — https://example.org/today — accessed: 2026-08-14
